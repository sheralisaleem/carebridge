# Departments and Doctor Directory

The Doctors page and live clinic scheduler provide doctor names, languages, telehealth details and current availability. Individual working hours can change as clinicians edit their schedules.

## General Medicine
General Medicine is the entry point for all new patients and handles check-ups, follow-ups and referrals.

## Pediatrics
Pediatrics sees patients under 16 years old.

## Cardiology
Cardiology requires a referral from a General Medicine doctor.

## Dermatology
Established patients can book directly without a referral. Availability and telehealth details depend on the doctor.

## Orthopedics
Established patients can book directly without a referral.

## Choosing a department
The assistant can describe which department a doctor belongs to but cannot decide which specialty a patient medically needs. A patient who is unsure should book General Medicine.
